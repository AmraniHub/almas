Almas – Privacy Policy

Package: com.almas.app  |  Effective date: June 1, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Almas Android application published under the package name com.almas.app. It explains how the current version of the app handles information.

2. Information We Collect

The current version of the Almas app does not require users to create an account or sign in.

The current version does not intentionally collect personal information from users for storage on Almas servers through the app itself.

When you use the app, the following information may be processed locally on your device for app functionality:

  • Product browsing state
  • Cart and order-draft state
  • Text typed into the in-app chat demo interface

In the current release this information is used only inside the app experience and is not sent to Almas through an in-app account or analytics system.

3. Network Use

The app uses internet access to load app content such as product images and related online resources.

Standard technical data may be processed by infrastructure involved in delivering online content — such as hosting providers, content delivery networks, Google Play, or your mobile carrier or internet provider. This may include information such as IP address, device or browser metadata, and request logs that are normally generated when online content is delivered.

4. External Services and Links

The app includes actions that can open third-party apps or services outside the Almas app, including:

  • Phone dialer
  • WhatsApp
  • Web browser
  • Waze or other map / navigation services

If you choose to use those actions, your interaction with those third-party services is governed by their own terms and privacy policies, not this Privacy Policy.

5. Notifications, Location, and Accounts

The current release of the Almas app does not require account login.

The current release does not request location access from the published Android manifest.

The current release does not request notification permission from the published Android manifest.

6. Data Sharing

The current release does not intentionally share user-provided personal information with third parties through an in-app account, advertising SDK, or analytics SDK.

We do not sell personal information.

7. Data Retention

Because the current release does not provide account-based data storage, Almas does not retain app account data for users through the mobile app.

Some temporary app state may remain on your device as part of normal app operation until the app is closed, the app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled.

8. Children

Almas is intended for business and professional use and is not directed to children.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to reduce unnecessary access to user data in the current release. However, no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date shown at the top of this page.
