How We Work

Seven steps.
Zero friction.

01

Confidential Intake

A private conversation — one question at a time — to understand your primary objectives, current health baseline, travel preferences, time available, investment range, privacy requirements, and prior interventions.

02

Clinical Profile Construction

We review any existing biomarker panels, imaging, genomic reports, and physician notes you choose to share, building a structured clinical profile that informs our matching.

03

Precision Matching

Our proprietary framework cross-references your profile against the clinical capabilities, diagnostic depth, therapeutic modalities, jurisdictional fit, and service standards of each verified facility.

04

Three-Option Presentation

We present exactly three recommendations — each with clinic name, location, signature program, investment range, and a precise explanation of why it fits your specific profile.

05

Logistics Coordination

Once you confirm, we coordinate directly with the facility: travel dates, party size, medical records transfer, pre-arrival protocols, and private transportation arrangements.

06

Pre-Arrival Briefing

Within 24 hours of confirmation, you receive a complete briefing: facility overview, physician team, program schedule, dietary preparation, and everything needed to arrive optimally prepared.

07

Longitudinal Follow-Up

Post-visit, we schedule biomarker retests, coordinate report reviews with your primary physician, and build the next phase of your multi-year protocol — tracking your biological trajectory over time.
