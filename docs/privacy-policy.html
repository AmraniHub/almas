Almas – Privacy Policy

Almas Spices & Herbs (معشبة ألماس), Ksar Sghir, Morocco · Package: com.almas.app · Effective date: October 27, 2026

1. Who we are

Almas is the app of Almas Spices & Herbs, a spice and herb shop in Ksar Sghir, near Tangier, Morocco. It shows the shop's products and prices and lets you send an order to the shop on WhatsApp. Contact: amranialabs@gmail.com.

2. No account, no tracking

The app has no account, no login and no password. It contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDK. We do not sell or rent personal information.

3. What stays on your phone

When you send an order, the app saves on your phone the name of your business (or your name), your phone number and your delivery address, so the next order is faster. It also keeps your cart and the last product catalogue it downloaded. This information stays on your phone: the app does not send it to any server of ours. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

4. What you send to the shop

When you tap to send an order, the app opens WhatsApp with a message you can read before sending: the products, quantities and totals, and the name, phone number, address and notes you typed. The shop receives it only if you send it. Messages on WhatsApp are governed by WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.

5. What the app downloads

The app downloads the shop's product list, prices and product photos from the shop's server (hosted by Cloudflare). These requests carry no personal information. Like any connection to a website, they can be recorded in the hosting provider's technical logs (for example the IP address and the time), which are used only to deliver and protect the service.

6. Other apps the app can open

The app can open your phone dialer to call the shop, WhatsApp to message it, and a maps app to show its location. What happens in those apps is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

7. Permissions

The app uses internet access only, to download the product list and photos. It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or notifications.

8. Children

The app is a shop catalogue intended for adults and businesses. It is not directed to children.

9. Security

The app talks to the shop's server over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Because your details are kept on your phone and not on our servers, protecting your phone with a screen lock protects them too.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

11. Contact

Questions or requests about your information: amranialabs@gmail.com.
