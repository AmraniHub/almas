Almas – Privacy Policy

Almas Spices & Herbs (معشبة ألماس), Ksar Sghir, Morocco · Website almas5.com · App com.almas.app · Effective date: November 16, 2026

1. Who we are

Almas Spices & Herbs is a spice and herb shop in Ksar Sghir, near Tangier, Morocco. This policy covers our website almas5.com and our Android app, which shows the same website. Contact: contact@almas5.com.

2. What we collect when you order

When you send an order, a business quote request or a standing order, we receive what you type in the form: your name (or your business name), your phone number, your delivery address, your notes, the products and quantities, and the payment method you choose. We use it to prepare and deliver your order, to contact you about it and to keep our sales records. It is stored in our order system (hosted by Cloudflare), copied to a private Google Sheet the shop uses, and sent to the shop owners as a message on Telegram. We do not sell or rent it.

3. Card payments

If you pay by card, the payment is handled by Stripe on Stripe's own page. We never see or store your card number; Stripe tells us only whether the order is paid. Stripe's privacy policy applies to the payment.

4. Loyalty points and invitations

Points are linked to the phone number you order with. We keep the points you earn and spend with each order. To show your points, the website asks for your phone number and one of your order numbers. If you invite a friend, we record the invitation code and whether it led to a first order, so both of you get your points. Points that are not used lapse 12 months after your last earning.

5. Reviews

If you leave a review, we publish the name you enter, your rating and your text. We check reviews before they appear.

6. The shopping assistant

If you ask the assistant a question, the question is sent to Google's Gemini service to write the answer. Please do not type personal details in it.

7. Analytics and advertising

The website uses Google Analytics and the Meta (Facebook) pixel. They set cookies or similar identifiers in your browser and record how the site is used: pages and products viewed, items added to the cart, checkout and orders (order number and value). We use this to understand what people look for, to measure our advertising on Facebook and Instagram, and to show our products to people who visited the site. Google signals and Google ad personalisation are turned off. Meta may match visits to Facebook and Instagram accounts, and may receive contact details typed on the page in a scrambled (hashed) form. When you send an order or a price request, or a card payment goes through, our server also tells Meta about it directly: the event, its number and value, the products, your phone number in hashed form, and your IP address and browser type. This lets us measure our advertising even when the pixel is blocked; Meta counts each event once. You can block these cookies in your browser settings, and manage ads in your Facebook and Google ad settings. The shop does not see who you are from this data.

8. What stays in your browser or phone

Your cart, your language and the details you typed for your last order are saved in your browser (or in the app), so the next order is faster. They stay on your device. You can remove them by clearing the site's data or the app's data.

9. Hosting and technical logs

The website is hosted by Vercel and our order system by Cloudflare. Like any website, they record technical logs (for example the IP address and the time of a request), used only to deliver and protect the service.

10. How long we keep information

We keep order records as long as we need them for our accounts and for the loyalty programme. Analytics data is kept for 14 months.

11. Your rights

Under Moroccan law 09-08, and the European GDPR if you are in the EU, you can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it or to delete it, and object to its use for advertising. Write to contact@almas5.com. You can also complain to the Moroccan data protection authority (CNDP).

12. The Android app

The app shows the almas5.com website, so everything above applies inside the app too. The app itself uses internet access only. It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or notifications. It can open your phone dialer, WhatsApp or a maps app when you tap those buttons; what happens there is governed by those apps' own terms.

13. Children

Our shop is intended for adults and businesses. It is not directed to children.

14. Security

The website, the app and our order system use encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to orders is limited to the shop owners.

15. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

16. Contact

Questions or requests about your information: contact@almas5.com.
